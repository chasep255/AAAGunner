FIELD GUIDE

How to play

Quick Start

Choose a difficulty, then click Deploy. Move the mouse to aim and hold the left button or Space to fire. Enable Aim-ahead indicator in settings for optional aiming help (off by default). With it enabled and a nearby plane in gun range, aim at the gold AIM HERE ring to allow for its movement and bullet drop. Brighter tracer streaks show where your rounds are going. Use missiles for distant planes. Planes spawn far out at different altitudes on left, center, and right approaches. They follow varied crossing, sweeping, weaving, and rising paths, line up for a head-on gun pass, then slow into a banked turn and circle back for another attack. Their routes stay in front of you and fit your viewport. They raise their noses to allow for bullet drop during gun passes. Their fixed forward guns fire only while lined up on your emplacement. Before firing, a plane must stay clearly inside your view with an unobstructed line of sight for 1.5 seconds. Leaving that area stops the rest of its burst; rounds already in flight continue and can miss. Watch for sustained wing muzzle flashes, short tracer streaks, FIRING markers, and nearby impact sparks. Shoot them down before they attack or release a bomb. Touch players can drag a finger inside the arena to aim and fire.

Controls

Stop: End the current round and unlock Difficulty. Change settings, then click Deploy. From the pause screen, choose Stop and change settings; this also exits fullscreen.
Aim-ahead indicator: Toggle the gold aim-here ring in the main or pause menu. It starts off and can be changed while paused without restarting.
P / Esc: Pause or resume.
R: Restart the round.
Sound: Toggle game audio.
Fullscreen: Expand the game. Controls also work in fullscreen.
Changing tabs or moving focus out of the window pauses the round.

Health

You start with 75 health, displayed as 100% when full. Each incoming round that hits deals damage, so a sustained burst is dangerous. Rounds must intersect your emplacement to cause damage; misses can strike the ground or water nearby. After five seconds without damage, health regenerates at four points per second, up to 75. Each new hit restarts that delay. The health panel shows incoming fire and recovery. Survive until the five-minute timer runs out; losing all health ends the round immediately. Pausing also freezes attacks, health recovery, and missile reloads.

Bombs

A plane that gets close can release a glide bomb once per pass. Listen for the alert and look for the orange BOMB marker and countdown. You have about four seconds to shoot it down for 50 points. A missile can intercept a bomb on contact, but heat seekers only lock onto aircraft. A nearby ground impact causes heavy blast damage; the closer the blast, the harder it hits. Planes rearm their bombs when they circle back. Bomb arcs and blast damage are fictional arcade rules.

Missiles and Flares

Aim near a plane until the missile panel shows LOCK. Right-click, press M, or tap Fire missile to launch. A missile destroys a plane with one hit. The magazine holds five missiles, which can be fired back-to-back. One missile replenishes every three seconds until the magazine is full. Missiles remain in flight until they strike a plane, bomb, flare, ground, or water, or travel more than 4 km from you. Firing without a lock sends it straight ahead.

The heat seeker can lock aircraft from the front as well as the rear. Missiles accelerate after launch and lead moving aircraft, but their turn rate is limited. A target that slips outside the seeker cone breaks the lock; a missed missile continues flying instead of making a tight loop back. Planes sometimes release glowing decoy flares that draw an approaching missile away. Higher difficulties use flares more often.

Heat and Scoring

The four cannons alternate fire at a combined 60 rounds per second after a brief feed startup. Each cannon has its own recoil and muzzle flash. Shots disperse around your aim, and the spread widens as the barrels heat. Short bursts keep the grouping tighter. Gun rounds reach up to 2 km; the optional gold sight ring estimates the lead and drop for the plane closest to your aim. Keep tracking the ring during a burst; sharp changes in the plane’s course can still spoil a shot. Sustained fire heats its barrels; overheating temporarily prevents firing until it cools. Release the trigger between bursts.

Planes take one or two HE gun hits. A green ring indicates one hit remaining. Destroy planes within five seconds of one another to build a multiplier up to ×5. A gap of more than five seconds between plane kills resets the streak. Bomb interceptions award a flat 50 points. A round lasts five minutes, with three 100-second waves that become progressively busier. The active aircraft limits for waves 1 / 2 / 3 are 4 / 5 / 7 on Relaxed, 5 / 7 / 8 on Arcade, and 8 / 9 / 10 on Frenzy. Four aircraft approach at deployment. Gun hit percentage counts rounds only; missiles are separate.

Simulation and Effects

The fictional quad mount fires 20×102 mm HEI-T rounds at 987 m/s with a G1 BC of 0.4933883. Velocity comes from MKE’s six-barrel cannon specification; BC and 103 g projectile mass come from its M56 A3 HEI-T listing. Each shell uses gravity and G1 drag in still air, and bursts on contact with an aircraft, terrain, or water. Yellow tracers show the flight path; impact flashes, smoke, fragments, and delayed explosion sounds show where rounds land. The quad mount, rate of fire, dispersion, 2 km shell boundary, and HE damage are game rules. Aircraft follow curved approaches with limited bank, roll, and pitch rates. Their guns fire along the aircraft’s forward direction. Missiles leave thin smoke trails that linger and disperse along their flight paths. Distant firing and explosion sounds are delayed by travel distance. Explosions produce expanding fireballs, drifting smoke, and falling debris. Published data: MKE product catalogue (manufacturer catalogue mirrored on Scribd). Aircraft, missile steering, flares, health, and heat use fictional arcade rules. Scores stay in memory for the current visit.
